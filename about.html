Why Raseedee exists

Raseedee started as a personal spreadsheet — a way to track every riyal earned and spent while rebuilding financially in Doha, Qatar. It grew into an automated system, then a real app.

That same discipline is now built for anyone in the Gulf who wants a clear, honest picture of their money — spending, savings, investments and net worth, in one bilingual dashboard, without wrestling with a bank app or building a spreadsheet from scratch.

Raseedee is built specifically for the Gulf and Middle East: Arabic and English, GCC currencies, and a product designed around how people here actually earn, spend and save — not a global app with a translation added on.
