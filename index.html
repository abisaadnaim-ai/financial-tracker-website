Meet Penny, your AI assistant

Penny lives in a chat bubble on every page, so you never have to dig through reports to get an answer. Ask it about your own money in plain English — or Arabic — and it looks at your real transactions and portfolio to reply.

  • ✓ Ask about your spending, any time range. "How much did I spend on dining last month?" or "What was my savings rate in 2025?" — not just a fixed recent window.
  • ✓ Ask about your portfolio. Holdings, current value, and unrealized gain or loss, computed with the exact same numbers as your Investments tab.
  • ✓ Ask "how do I…" questions. Penny explains the app and can take you straight to the right screen.
  • ✓ Private by design. Penny can only ever see your own account's data — never anyone else's — and doesn't remember the conversation after you close it.
