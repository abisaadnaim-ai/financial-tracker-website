Investments & portfolio

Optional add-on module for tracking a real investment portfolio alongside your everyday finances.

Asset categories

Track stocks, ETFs, mutual funds, bonds, cryptocurrency, commodities, fixed deposits, cash/currencies, real estate, vehicles, and other assets — each asset carries its own identifier (ticker, ISIN, fund code, or crypto symbol) where one applies.

Activity log

Record buys, sells, deposits, and transfers on the Activity tab. Every investment transaction that moves cash is automatically kept in sync with your Transactions log, so your net worth never double-counts or drifts out of sync.

Prices — live or manual

Priced assets can pull live market prices, or you can enter a price manually for anything without live coverage. A "Refresh all" action updates every live-priced position in one click.

Portfolio allocation

The Portfolio tab shows total invested, current value, and unrealized gain/loss across every currency you hold, converted to USD. Click any slice of the allocation pie to open a category breakdown — including your Savings balance, shown alongside your cash-equivalent holdings under "Currencies."

Multi-currency by design

Hold a US stock, a European fund, and a local currency account at the same time — every total on the Portfolio tab is shown per-currency and consolidated in USD, so nothing gets lost in translation.

Turning it on

Investments & portfolio is available as an add-on on any paid plan. If your account doesn't have it yet, the Investments tab will show pricing details and next steps.
