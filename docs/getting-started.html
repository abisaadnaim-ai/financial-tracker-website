Getting started

Raseedee takes about two minutes to set up. Here's the order that works best.

1. Create your account

Sign up with your email. You'll land on an empty Dashboard — that's expected, it fills in as soon as you log your first transaction.

2. Set your preferred currency

In Profile settings, choose the currency you think in day-to-day. Every dashboard total is shown in this currency, and investments in other currencies are converted automatically alongside it.

3. Add your income and expense fields

Under Settings → Fields, create the fields that match how you actually earn and spend — e.g. "Salary" and "Freelance" for income, "Rent," "Groceries," and "Transport" for expenses. Each field is tagged Needs/Wants (expense) or Revenue/Saving/Investment (income) so your reports stay meaningful. Free plans include 1 income and 10 expense fields; see Plans & pricing for higher limits.

4. Log your first transactions

Add a transaction: pick the field, enter the amount and date, add an optional description. Your Dashboard, Transactions log, and net worth all update immediately.

5. (Optional) Add savings and investments

Savings are tracked as a dedicated field right from Transactions — no separate setup needed. Investment/portfolio tracking is a separate add-on; see Investments & portfolio to learn how it works.

6. Install it on your phone

Raseedee is a installable web app (PWA). On mobile, open it in your browser and choose "Add to Home Screen" for an app-like experience with optional reminder notifications.
